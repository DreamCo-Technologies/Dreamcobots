# Buddy full-fleet execution and specialist portfolio operating model

## Canonical structure

DreamCo retains 45 canonical business divisions. The ten supplemental portfolios remain visible under their source identities, but each has one canonical operating owner. They are specialist capability lanes, not duplicate primary divisions:

| Specialist portfolio | Canonical owner | Exclusive operating domain |
|---|---|---|
| DreamAffiliate | DreamSalesPro | Affiliate program operations |
| DreamAgency | DreamProServices | Agency delivery operations |
| DreamFoundry | DreamAIInfra | Model productization and evaluation |
| DreamFranchise | DreamBizLaunch | Franchise system design and governance |
| DreamIntegrations | DreamCodeLab | Cross-system integration reliability |
| DreamLicensing | DreamLegal | Commercial IP rights administration |
| DreamRobotics | DreamAIInfra | Physical robotics task and safety operations |
| DreamSaaS | DreamAutomation | SaaS product and subscription lifecycle |
| DreamStreaming | DreamContent | Streaming channel and broadcast operations |
| DreamTravel | DreamTransport | Traveler journey planning and support |

Each specialist portfolio has five existing bot identities. The charter expands each bot into an implementable scope, including capability requirements, approved source classes, monetization options, and the evidence event that would count as a realized outcome. Existing canonical divisions retain their primary ownership. Collaborator links are secondary, not a second owner.

The repository also has 112 GrokTeammates profiles. These are shared workforce lanes, not additional business divisions; each is routed to a platform MasterBot based on its lane description while remaining available across domain divisions. Nineteen Three.js Markdown-only profiles remain in an explicit UNASSIGNED review queue, with DreamCodeLab and DreamArts suggested for owner review. The generated division-manifest count of 57 therefore represents 45 canonical domains + 10 specialist portfolios + 1 shared teammate group + 1 unassigned review bucket, not 57 equivalent business divisions.

## Full catalog execution map

config/generated/buddy-fleet-execution-map.json is generated from the complete config/bots/bot-manifests.generated.json catalog, not just the App_bots directory. Every profile receives:

- one canonical division owner or a DreamDiscovery review route;
- one primary MasterBot route, plus access to shared platform MasterBots 46–65;
- a Buddy governed-queue, learning, benchmark and failure-recovery route;
- source paths, declared integrations and capability lineage;
- either a commercial opportunity-to-cash plan or an internal capability/value-delivery route;
- explicit duplicate-profile and duplicate-capability review signals.

Commercial candidates receive a revenue lane hypothesis, approved source categories, buyer/value hypothesis, offer and fulfillment sequence, required approval points, and a verified revenue event. A generated plan is not a live bot, a customer, or a sale.

## Opportunity-to-cash controls

Money OS opportunity sources must be official, licensed, authorized, or user-supplied with permission. The route is:

1. Discover from an approved source.
2. Verify source freshness, rights, terms and eligibility.
3. Score fit, value, cost, risk and confidence.
4. Prepare a distinct offer and fulfillment plan.
5. Simulate unit economics and downside.
6. Request required owner approval for outreach, spend, contracts and payments.
7. Execute only through approved connectors and bounded permissions.
8. Record the verified conversion, accepted lead, paid invoice or successful billing event.
9. Send outcome and failure evidence to Buddy for reviewed learning.

Forecasts and simulated sales are never revenue. Declared API names are integration targets, not proof that credentials or live provider connections exist. No bot is live-enabled by this generator.

## Learning and mastery

Buddy is the orchestrator, not a blanket permission grant. Each bot's learning loop should consume only sanitized, rights-cleared and provenance-backed examples. Promotion requires reproducible test evidence, benchmark holdouts, review and lineage. Failures create structured recovery tasks; they do not silently modify production code or model weights.

## Coverage discrepancy being corrected

The generated bot manifest currently contains 1,232 profiles while the older revenue-readiness artifact covered 1,051 App_bots entries. The revenue-readiness builder now enumerates the full canonical generated manifest. Profiles missing business or sandbox curriculum remain blocked in readiness until those curricula and runtime evidence exist; they are not silently treated as passed.

## What this work does not claim

The charter and execution map are planning and routing artifacts. They do not prove runtime implementation, connected credentials, passing benchmarks, legal eligibility, customer demand, production readiness, model training or realized revenue. Live monetization remains gated by config/live-revenue-gate.json and explicit owner authorization.
