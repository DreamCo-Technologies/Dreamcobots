# Global AI Research Observatory

DreamCo treats AI research as a continuously refreshed evidence system.

## Source families

- model releases and technical reports
- peer-reviewed research and preprints
- open-source repositories and implementations
- model cards and dataset documentation
- benchmark releases and evaluation harnesses
- RL/RLVR and post-training research
- distillation and synthetic-data research
- reasoning and test-time-compute research
- MoE, attention, quantization, and inference optimization
- distributed training and systems research
- agent/tool-use research
- safety, security, and evaluation research
- AI education and curriculum research
- hardware, compiler, and serving optimization
- international research across major AI ecosystems

## Evidence ladder

`Observed -> Reported -> Reproduced -> Benchmarked -> Verified -> Adopted`

A paper, model card, repository, article, or benchmark report is evidence to investigate—not automatic proof that a technique works for DreamCo.

## Source scoring

Prioritize sources by authority, freshness, benchmark relevance, active capability-gap coverage, transfer value, and cost efficiency. Prefer primary/official sources when available and authorized. Deduplicate content and deprioritize stale, low-authority, restricted, or rights-uncertain material.

## Learning pipeline

`source change -> claim extraction -> capability mapping -> original lesson -> sandbox exercise -> benchmark -> independent holdout -> regression -> promotion`

DreamCo should create original learning artifacts rather than copying protected source material.

## Research questions

For each useful technique, ask:

1. What capability does it improve?
2. Can DreamCo reproduce it legally and safely?
3. What data, compute, memory, latency, and monetary resources does it require?
4. Does it improve the 500-model comparison or Buddy's capability baseline?
5. Does it reduce token or retrieval cost without unacceptable capability loss?
6. Does it generalize to held-out tasks?
7. Can the useful behavior be distilled into a reusable capability?

## Global comparison

U.S. frontier methods and international efficiency methods are both research inputs. Buddy can select Frontier, Efficiency, Compare, or Hybrid mode. Country of origin is not the selection criterion; reproducible evidence is.

## Open-source policy

Publish DreamCo code, schemas, benchmarks, evaluation tools, and experiment metadata when licensing permits. Respect third-party licenses and never publish credentials, private data, encryption keys, restricted prompts, or protected benchmark answers.
