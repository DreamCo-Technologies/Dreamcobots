# Buddy Reality Simulation Engine

Buddy treats realism as an engineering target with measurable evidence, not a marketing claim.

## Reality loop

`observe → model → simulate → compare → score → identify gap → rebuild → retest → preserve regression`

## Benchmark dimensions

Visual fidelity, physics, human behavior, NPC intelligence, world consistency, audio, animation, environmental dynamics, domain accuracy, interaction, performance, stability, and accessibility.

## Ground truth hierarchy

1. Physical measurements or authoritative real-world data.
2. Professional/domain expert rubrics.
3. Repeatable controlled scenarios.
4. Cross-engine comparisons.
5. User/playtest measurements.
6. Estimated judgment when stronger evidence is unavailable.

Every score records its evidence level and uncertainty.
