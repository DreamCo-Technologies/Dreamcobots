Automatic Repair Rules
Buddy should be allowed to propose and test low-risk repairs such as fixing documented imports, improving test fixtures, updating compatible dependencies, and repairing broken internal links.
It should require approval before destructive changes, financial transactions, publishing sensitive information, changing production permissions, or performing consequential real-world actions.
Every repair should be reversible and tested before promotion.
