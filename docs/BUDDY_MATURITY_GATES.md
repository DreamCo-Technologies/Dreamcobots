# Buddy Maturity Gates

A capability should advance only when evidence supports the next level.

## Gate 1 — Understand
Requirements, constraints, risks and unknowns are documented.

## Gate 2 — Explore
Repository, dependencies, integrations and relevant prior failures are identified.

## Gate 3 — Plan
The best specialist route and acceptance tests are selected.

## Gate 4 — Build
Implementation follows repository conventions and keeps changes reversible.

## Gate 5 — Test
Unit, integration, regression and task-specific tests provide evidence.

## Gate 6 — Review
Security, dependencies, UX/accessibility and performance are reviewed when relevant.

## Gate 7 — Benchmark
Quality, correctness, speed, safety and reliability are measured against versioned thresholds.

## Gate 8 — Release
Artifacts are reproducible, documented, monitored and recoverable.

## Gate 9 — Learn
Failures and successes are converted into lessons and regression coverage.

## Gate 10 — Mature
The capability remains stable across repeated runs and changing conditions.

### Rule
No gate may be skipped merely because a feature is exciting, urgent or large. A gate may be simplified for low-risk changes, but the evidence requirement remains proportional to impact.
