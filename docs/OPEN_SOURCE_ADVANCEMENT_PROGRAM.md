# DreamCo Open-Source Advancement Program

DreamCo should not define success as reproducing an existing capability. The goal is to learn from the state of the art, independently rebuild useful behavior, measure it, and then attempt to advance it.

## The advancement ladder

```text
Existing capability
      ↓
Behavior specification
      ↓
License/provenance review
      ↓
DreamCo test suite
      ↓
Independent implementation
      ↓
Baseline benchmark
      ↓
Gap analysis
      ↓
New hypothesis
      ↓
Sandbox experiment
      ↓
Improvement benchmark
      ↓
Holdout + transfer + regression
      ↓
Document result
      ↓
Open-source release/upstream contribution when permitted
```

## Music research goals

DreamCo's own research targets should include:

- controllable composition
- long-form musical coherence
- arrangement editing
- stem-aware generation and revision
- expressive performance
- production-quality mixing/mastering assistance
- synchronized scoring for video
- low-cost generation and iteration
- better human-preference evaluation

## Video research goals

DreamCo's own research targets should include:

- persistent characters across scenes
- controllable cinematography
- multi-shot narrative coherence
- temporal consistency
- synchronized dialogue/music/effects
- editable generated scenes
- deterministic or reproducible revisions
- efficient generation
- automated continuity checking
- professional post-production assistance

## What counts as advancement

A project advances the state of the art when it produces measurable improvement in at least one meaningful dimension while maintaining or improving important neighboring dimensions.

Examples:

- better quality at the same compute
- same quality at lower compute
- better consistency at the same quality
- better controllability without unacceptable quality loss
- improved human preference
- improved reliability on difficult holdouts
- improved accessibility for open-source developers

A benchmark win that causes serious regression elsewhere does not count as a complete advancement.
