# DreamCo Global Government Improvement Program

DreamCo treats government improvement as an evidence-driven operating program, not a claim that one country or agency model applies everywhere.

## Scope

The system maps jurisdictions from authoritative country/area standards, then discovers each jurisdiction's actual government levels, departments/agencies, programs, services, forms, fees, jobs, data/APIs, budgets, spending, debt, procurement, grants and performance information.

Government functions are normalized through a COFOG-aligned map for comparison, while local names, powers, laws, fees, eligibility and accounting definitions remain source-specific.

## Department-by-department loop

For every discovered department:

1. Verify mandate and authoritative sources.
2. Inventory public services and internal workflows.
3. Inventory jobs and task families.
4. Inventory forms, documents, fees, channels, data, APIs, systems and vendors.
5. Inventory budget/spending/procurement/grant sources.
6. Measure service friction, staff workload, errors, backlogs, accessibility and processing time where data exists.
7. Compare equivalent functions in peer jurisdictions.
8. Generate parallel benchmark, builder, integration, QA, security/privacy, accessibility and release-review workers.
9. Prototype improvements in sandbox.
10. Measure before/after results.
11. Pilot only with authorized users and rollback/observability.
12. Preserve evidence, limitations and source freshness.

## Citizen service goal

Buddy should make government easier to navigate without fabricating authority. It can help discover services, collect required documents, draft forms, explain official requirements, calculate fees from current official sources, track deadlines/status where authorized, support renewals, organize appeals-navigation information, and reduce repeated data entry with permission.

It must not decide eligibility, invent program rules or fees, impersonate officials, bypass government portals, or make consequential government decisions reserved for authorized humans.

## Public money transparency

DreamCo tracks official public debt, revenue, budgets, spending, contracts, grants and related fiscal records when authoritative sources expose them. Every displayed value keeps jurisdiction, currency, accounting definition, period/source date and freshness.

A daily scheduler may check every configured source for new records, but DreamCo never manufactures a daily number for a source that publishes monthly, quarterly or annually.

Citizen views should answer: where money came from, where it went, which department/program used it, who received contracts/grants where public, how the value changed, and how fresh the source is.

## Economic development

Local economic-development benchmarking covers employer/industry mix, business formation, retention/expansion, supplier/manufacturer attraction, workforce skills, infrastructure, housing, vacant/underused property, redevelopment, incentives/grants, procurement leakage, local purchasing, tax-base trends, capital projects, permitting/zoning friction, entrepreneurship resources and financing pathways.

The output is a measurable plan, not a promise of economic growth.

## Housing and real estate

Housing intelligence can organize authoritative foreclosure/auction sources, tax sales, land-bank inventory, public surplus property, government housing and down-payment programs, rehab resources, zoning/permits, taxes/liens where public, development incentives and affordable-housing resources.

Terms such as "cheap", "no money down", eligibility or available inventory require current source evidence. Professional/legal review remains necessary where applicable.

## Gap closure

Every generated benchmark artifact is ingested by the Parallel Benchmark Gap Registry. Each case gets accountable parallel roles and cannot close without baseline evidence, acceptance criteria, tests, security/privacy review where applicable, before/after scores and runtime evidence.
