# Buddy Tolkien Studies and Mythopoeia Track

This curriculum teaches Tolkien-adjacent academic skills—philology, comparative mythology, language construction, worldbuilding systems, literary criticism, and adaptation analysis—without copying or training on copyrighted Tolkien texts.

## Rights boundary

Tolkien's novels, appendices, letters, and many modern editions/translations are copyrighted. The track blocks ingestion and model training on copyrighted Tolkien text by default. It permits bibliographic metadata, factual context, and authorized short scholarly summaries; each academic source and each translation must pass an item-level rights review. User-supplied text requires a specific permission attestation.

## Curriculum

1. **Philology and language history:** sound change, Old English, Old Norse, morphology, semantics, and orthography.
2. **Comparative mythology:** mythic motifs, oral traditions, cosmology, heroic cycles, and cultural memory.
3. **Constructed-language design:** phonology, morphology, syntax, lexicon, and writing systems.
4. **Worldbuilding systems:** geography, climate, ecology, institutions, genealogy, and chronology.
5. **Narrative architecture:** point of view, theme, moral conflict, character agency, and epic structure.
6. **Literary criticism:** close reading, historical context, evidence, citations, and reception.
7. **Adaptation analysis:** visual storytelling, sound, game narrative, and adaptation theory.
8. **Original mythopoeia:** create original cosmologies, cultures, languages, maps, and stories with an originality review.

A curriculum plan is not evidence of study or mastery. Benchmark texts remain separate from training materials. No bulk downloading, verbatim reproduction, copyrighted corpus ingestion, or imitation of Tolkien's distinctive protected expression.
