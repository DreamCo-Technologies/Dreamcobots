# Buddy Tolkien Studies Track

## Purpose

This is a scholarly study curriculum for Buddy, not a Tolkien-text ingestion or model-training corpus. It teaches philological method, historical linguistics, mythopoeia, literary analysis, archival research, digital humanities and source criticism.

The track is registered in `config/buddy-study-resources-1101-1200-expansion.json` under `specialized_study_tracks` with stable ID `tolkien`. Its eight lessons reference existing catalog resources 1176–1189. The catalog is a discovery index: it does not prove that Buddy has read or mastered the material.

## Eight-lesson sequence

1. **Scholarly context and philology** — build a cited timeline from authorized biographical and archival metadata.
2. **Historical linguistics** — compare phonology, morphology and language-family concepts using independently licensed linguistic datasets.
3. **Mythopoeia and comparative mythology** — write an original comparative analysis using public-domain myths and peer-reviewed scholarship.
4. **Narrative architecture** — study high-level literary structures and create an original worldbuilding schema without reusing protected characters, settings, languages or distinctive expression.
5. **Archives and provenance** — create bibliographic records with edition, archive, rights status and citation trail.
6. **Digital humanities** — model public catalog metadata as a knowledge graph with citations and uncertainty labels.
7. **Reception and criticism** — compare peer-reviewed interpretations and record evidence, scope and limitations.
8. **Capstone** — produce an original, fully cited research brief and pass provenance, originality and rights review.

## Approved study inputs

- Public bibliographic metadata and archival catalog records.
- Secondary scholarship only where the individual article's license and terms permit the intended use.
- Public-domain comparative literature after checking jurisdiction and edition.
- Original DreamCo notes and exercises.

## Rights and safety boundaries

The Tolkien Estate's official FAQ states that published quotations require permission through HarperCollins; Tolkien artworks, maps, scripts and graphic works are protected; invented languages and scripts are protected; public readings require permission and may not be recorded or distributed; and it does not permit materials referring to characters, stories, places, events or other elements from Tolkien works. Marquette separately states that the Estate retains literary rights and copyright to the manuscripts, which may be reproduced or published only with written permission; its Anduin manuscript scans are accessible only on-site. Accordingly, this track is limited to scholarly study of Tolkien's life, philology, historical context, bibliography, criticism and reception—not fan fiction or story/character/world generation:

- Tolkien primary texts, scans, audio, audiobook files and transcripts are **not approved training data**.
- A public URL, search result, library catalog entry or open-access article label is not a training license.
- Do not reproduce invented languages/scripts, characters, settings, stories or distinctive protected expression. Do not generate materials referring to Tolkien characters, stories, places, events or other elements from Tolkien works under this track.
- Model-weight training is disabled. Any future permission request must be reviewed and recorded per item before use.
- Unknown rights are **BLOCKED**.
- Lessons may produce original scholarly commentary with citations, but may not reproduce substantial protected text.

## Evidence required for completion

For every lesson record: resource IDs, source URLs, access date, license/rights review, original artifact hash, learning objectives, rubric scores, reviewer, and completion status. A catalog entry, metadata fetch or generated plan is not proof of learning. Buddy must complete the exercise and pass the rubric before the lesson is marked mastered.
