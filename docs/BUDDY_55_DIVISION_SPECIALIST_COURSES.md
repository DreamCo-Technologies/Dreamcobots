# 55 division specialist course drafts

This curriculum pack turns the canonical `DIVISION_DOMAIN_PROFILES` into 55 original specialist course drafts. Every division keeps its own purpose, users, outcome, boundary, and focus areas. Each focus area receives an ordered specialist unit with learning objectives, source/tool discovery, workflow mapping, a synthetic lab, assessment, and evidence requirements.

## Course structure

- Shared prerequisites: AI literacy, research/evidence, privacy/security, and task-specific prerequisites.
- One specialist unit per canonical focus area.
- One end-to-end synthetic capstone per division.
- Human review, source provenance, and unseen transfer are required before any mastery claim.

## Ownership boundary

The course is authored for each canonical division profile, not assigned to a MasterBot. The separate 55-to-65 crosswalk remains proposed and requires owner approval before specialist routing or privileged task execution.

## Completion boundary

All courses and units remain drafts. The pack does not contain verified professional procedures for every jurisdiction, completed labs, scored results, or learner mastery.
