## Non-negotiable safety requirements

- No model is labeled best or verified without relevant independent benchmark evidence.
- Distinguish study, retrieval-augmented generation, and actual weight training.
- Respect dataset/model licenses, copyright, privacy, recording consent, and source access restrictions.
- Do not silently incur paid provider or infrastructure usage.
- Do not assume Wi-Fi means internet access or claim offline support without testing.
- Do not claim hardware compatibility without checking actual device capabilities.
- Do not merge unverified PRs or weaken CI/security gates.
- Investigate Cloudflare production deployment triggers and environment isolation before unrestricted repository writes, workflow dispatches, merges, or production deployment.
- Preserve authorization, branch protection, telemetry, audit logs, and rollback evidence.
- Keep simulated, unavailable, untested, and verified features visibly distinct.
