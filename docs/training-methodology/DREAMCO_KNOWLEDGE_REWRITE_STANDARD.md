# DreamCo Knowledge Synthesis & Rewrite Standard

## Goal
Convert diverse source material into concise, original DreamCo learning assets that are easier for humans to select and for AI systems to practice from.

## Core principle
**Learn from sources; synthesize into DreamCo knowledge.** A source is evidence/input, not a document to reproduce.

## Pipeline
1. Identify the source and its provenance.
2. Extract concepts, claims, methods, examples, disagreements, and limitations.
3. Cross-check important claims against independent sources when practical.
4. Normalize terminology into the DreamCo capability ontology.
5. Compress the material into original explanations, structured notes, procedures, questions, and practice tasks.
6. Add DreamCo's analysis: what we agree with, reject, improve, or still need to test.
7. Generate human-readable lessons and machine-readable skill objects separately.
8. Generate benchmark tasks and transfer tasks from the synthesized concepts.
9. Attach provenance and rights metadata to every derived asset.
10. Evaluate the derived material for factual accuracy, duplication risk, and learning usefulness.

## Output layers
### Human layer
- Short explanation
- Key ideas
- Practical examples
- Pros/cons
- Common mistakes
- Decision guide
- Further reading

### Machine layer
- Concepts
- Preconditions
- Procedures
- Constraints
- Counterexamples
- Skills
- Practice tasks
- Evaluation rubrics
- Transfer tasks
- Failure labels

## Originality rule
DreamCo assets should be independently expressed and synthesized. Do not reproduce substantial copyrighted passages, chapters, books, videos, or proprietary datasets. Summarization and transformation must respect applicable copyright, licensing, attribution, and contractual restrictions.

## Book analogy
A legitimate analogy is: authors research prior work, learn from it, synthesize ideas, and publish their own expression and analysis. DreamCo should follow that model rather than copying source works.

## Training-data products
A commercial package should preferentially contain DreamCo-owned or properly licensed synthesized lessons, task specifications, annotations, evaluations, and provenance—not unauthorized copies of source content.

## Quality gates
Every synthesized asset should answer:
- What sources informed it?
- What did DreamCo add or conclude?
- Can the learner understand it without the source?
- Can the learner practice it?
- Can improvement be measured?
- Is the asset legally permitted for its intended use?
- Is the wording sufficiently original?
