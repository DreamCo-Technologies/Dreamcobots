# DACL Customer Training Offer

## Positioning
DreamCo Adaptive Capability Learning (DACL) is a measurable AI training and evaluation service. Customers start with a baseline, receive a capability-gap map, train against targeted tasks, and receive independent post-training evaluation.

## Engagements
### 1. Capability Audit
Deliver baseline scorecard, gap map, benchmark plan, and prioritized training roadmap.

### 2. 30-Day Capability Sprint
Run targeted curriculum, sandbox practice, transfer tests, and weekly scorecard updates.

### 3. Continuous Learning Program
Maintain recurring evaluation, remediation, regression testing, and resource-efficiency optimization.

### 4. Enterprise Private Lab
Customer-specific benchmarks, private data boundaries, governance controls, audit trails, and custom reporting.

## Reportable outcomes
- Baseline score
- Post-training score
- Absolute and percentage-point delta
- Transfer-task score
- Regression score
- Failure categories before/after
- Training hours and compute used
- Cost per verified improvement
- Remaining capability gaps

## Commercial rule
Never promise a specific benchmark result before measurement. Price the infrastructure, training program, evaluation, and reporting; let verified outcomes demonstrate value.
