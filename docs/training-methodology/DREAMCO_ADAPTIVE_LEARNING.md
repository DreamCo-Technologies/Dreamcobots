# DreamCo Adaptive Capability Learning (DACL)

## Purpose
DACL is DreamCo's evidence-driven training methodology for improving an AI system's capabilities through continuous measurement, targeted practice, sandbox evaluation, failure analysis, remediation, and repeatable verification.

It may be marketed as a **frontier-style learning architecture**. It must not be represented as proof that Buddy is an AGI or frontier model unless independent evaluations establish that claim.

## Learning loop
1. Observe capability and benchmark evidence.
2. Build a capability graph and identify the highest-value gap.
3. Generate targeted curriculum from the gap.
4. Train or practice in an isolated sandbox.
5. Evaluate with reproducible tasks.
6. Analyze failures and classify the cause.
7. Select the next intervention.
8. Retest against the same and transfer tasks.
9. Record evidence, score delta, cost, latency, and regression state.
10. Promote only repeatable improvements; otherwise keep the capability in remediation.

## Adaptive mechanisms
- Difficulty progression based on demonstrated competence.
- Interleaving across related capabilities.
- Retrieval and spaced re-evaluation.
- Counterexample and adversarial practice.
- Failure clustering and targeted remediation.
- Transfer tests to detect narrow memorization.
- Regression suites after every material improvement.
- Resource-aware scheduling that prioritizes expected capability gain per unit of compute/time.
- Human review gates for high-impact or ambiguous outcomes.

## Evidence model
A training run is not mastery. Mastery requires reproducible task evidence, target attainment, regression checks, and repeatability across evaluation attempts.

## Productization
DACL can be packaged as:
- **DACL Core** — assessment, gap analysis, curriculum generation, and scorecards.
- **DACL Sandbox** — isolated training and benchmark environments.
- **DACL Fleet** — parallel training orchestration for many agents/divisions.
- **DACL Coach** — adaptive curriculum and failure remediation.
- **DACL Enterprise** — private benchmark suites, governance, audit trails, and integrations.
- **DACL Benchmark Lab** — customer capability evaluation and before/after reports.

## Customer deliverables
Each customer program should produce a baseline, prioritized capability map, training plan, evidence ledger, score deltas, regression report, resource-efficiency report, and final evaluation package.

## Claims policy
Do not sell benchmark activity as intelligence. Sell the measurable process and outcomes: repeatable evaluation, targeted improvement, transfer testing, regression resistance, and resource efficiency. Avoid unsupported AGI, autonomy, or frontier-model claims.
