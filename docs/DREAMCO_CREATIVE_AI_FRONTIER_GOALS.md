# DreamCo Creative AI Frontier Goals

DreamCo's objective is not to reproduce another company's product. The objective is to use the ecosystem as a source of ideas, research, interfaces, benchmarks, and lessons, then engineer original open-source systems that solve harder problems.

## Music goals

1. End-to-end professional music production from brief to mastered deliverable.
2. Strong controllability over structure, genre, instrumentation, emotion, tempo, and arrangement.
3. Editable stems and production-aware generation.
4. Consistent vocal/performance identity for authorized or fictional characters.
5. Automatic mixing and mastering with measurable targets.
6. Music-video synchronization and adaptive scoring.
7. Long-form album consistency without repetitive templates.
8. Human-in-the-loop producer workflows that preserve creative control.
9. Benchmark-driven improvement on composition, audio quality, preference, controllability, and originality.
10. Open tooling that lets developers reproduce and improve the pipeline.

## Film and video goals

1. Script-to-storyboard-to-shot planning.
2. Persistent characters, environments, props, costumes, and scene state.
3. Director-style control of camera, blocking, lighting, lens, focus, and pacing.
4. Multi-shot and long-form narrative consistency.
5. Audio, dialogue, music, and picture synchronization.
6. Automated editing, sound design, color, captions, and localization.
7. Original fictional actors and authorized digital performers with provenance.
8. Automated continuity checking.
9. Benchmark-driven quality improvement across technical and cinematic dimensions.
10. Developer-accessible open pipelines instead of opaque one-click generation only.

## Research-to-original-system loop

```text
Study ecosystem
  ↓
Record source + license + idea
  ↓
Extract transferable concept
  ↓
Write DreamCo design hypothesis
  ↓
Implement independently
  ↓
Build tests
  ↓
Benchmark against baseline
  ↓
Find gap
  ↓
Experiment in sandbox
  ↓
Keep measurable improvement
  ↓
Publish evidence and reusable tooling
```

## Frontier metric

DreamCo should measure progress using multiple axes rather than a single leaderboard number:

- quality %
- task success %
- controllability %
- consistency %
- generalization %
- human preference %
- reliability %
- safety %
- cost efficiency %
- latency efficiency %
- openness/reproducibility %

A frontier goal is considered meaningful only when the improvement is reproducible and survives holdout and regression evaluation.

## Open-source advancement rule

Once a capability is mastered, DreamCo does not simply move on. It asks what can be improved: lower cost, better quality, better controllability, better accessibility, better reproducibility, faster inference, better evaluation, or a genuinely new capability.
