# DreamCo Flagship Go-To-Market: Build Your Own AI

## Positioning

Primary message:

> **Build an AI that learns how you work.**

Supporting message:

> Give it your authorized knowledge, teach it your skills, and let Buddy help it improve while you stay in control.

Do not lead with model names, bot counts, infrastructure, or technical jargon.

## The sales story

### Problem

Most people use a generic AI that knows little about their specific life, work, preferences, files, or processes.

### Transformation

DreamCo turns generic AI access into a personal intelligence workspace:

**Your AI + your knowledge + your memory + your skills + your tools.**

### Proof moment

The customer should see their AI use one of their own approved sources and complete a useful task during onboarding.

### Expansion

Once the customer sees value, offer:

- more knowledge capacity
- more media processing
- more memory
- more skills
- more Superbots
- more automation
- premium model access
- business/team features
- export/deployment options

## Conversion funnel

```text
Curiosity
  -> 30-second demo
  -> create free Personal AI
  -> first personalized result
  -> teach one skill
  -> verify improvement
  -> paid upgrade
  -> invite/share
  -> build more AIs
  -> marketplace
```

The funnel must measure each step instead of assuming conversion.

## Product-led growth loops

### 1. Show-your-AI loop

A user can create a safe public demo card showing what their Personal AI can do without exposing private memory or source data.

### 2. Skill marketplace loop

Users can publish eligible, rights-cleared skill packages for others to discover, buy, remix, or use as templates.

### 3. Template loop

Create one-click starting templates:

- Student AI
- Job Coach AI
- Small Business AI
- Sales AI
- Real Estate AI
- Mechanic AI
- Creator AI
- Family Organizer AI
- Coding AI
- Research AI
- Customer Support AI
- Teacher AI

Templates are starting points, not claims that the AI has professional credentials.

### 4. Referral loop

A user receives a referral link for inviting another person. Reward design must be configured in the billing system and must never fabricate earnings or promise guaranteed income.

### 5. Build-in-public loop

For developers, GitHub makes the architecture inspectable. Publish reproducible demos, tests, benchmark evidence, and changelogs rather than unsupported performance claims.

## Offer design

Do not force every customer into the same package.

Use a capability-based catalog where each plan has explicit entitlements for:

- Personal AI count
- storage
- media processing
- memory
- skills
- Superbots
- automation
- model tiers
- premium model budget
- integrations
- seats

Prices are configuration, not hard-coded product truth. A pricing experiment must be labeled as an experiment until validated.

## Upgrade timing

Avoid showing a payment wall before the customer understands the product.

Good upgrade moments:

- after first personalized result
- after the AI passes its first benchmark
- when a user reaches a capacity limit
- when a requested skill requires a paid capability
- when a user asks for premium model access
- when a user wants to publish/deploy/share an AI

Buddy should explain exactly what changes with the upgrade.

## Buddy sales assistant

Buddy may:

1. explain plans;
2. compare capabilities;
3. estimate expected usage based on known plan limits;
4. explain model/resource costs where available;
5. prepare checkout;
6. ask for explicit confirmation;
7. hand off to the authenticated payment flow;
8. verify the entitlement after payment;
9. begin onboarding.

Buddy must not silently purchase, silently upgrade, invent prices, claim guaranteed savings, or claim a payment succeeded without backend evidence.

## Demonstration script

### 15-second version

> "Instead of giving you another generic chatbot, DreamCo lets you build your own AI. Tell Buddy what you want it to do, give it authorized knowledge, teach it a skill, and watch it improve."

### 60-second version

> "Start by telling Buddy what your AI is for. Add a few files, photos, recordings, or other authorized sources. Buddy organizes the information, helps you teach a skill, and gives you a test so you can see whether the AI actually improved. The AI can route different jobs to different engines while keeping your personal knowledge and skills in your DreamCo workspace."

## Trust-based sales rules

The product should sell through demonstration and evidence:

- show the user's own approved data working;
- show before/after task results;
- show usage and cost;
- show permissions;
- show what the AI does not know;
- show export/delete controls;
- show benchmark history.

Never use fake customer counts, fake revenue, fake model scores, fake testimonials, or fake scarcity.

## Marketplace strategy

Long-term marketplace categories:

- Personal AI templates
- Skills
- Workflows
- Tool connectors
- Knowledge packages with clear rights
- Evaluation packs
- Industry starter kits
- Business playbooks
- Training curricula

Every listing should carry provenance, license/rights status, version, compatibility, benchmark evidence, support status, and price.

## Customer retention

The AI should become more useful over time without becoming a black box.

Retention features:

- weekly improvement report
- new skill suggestions
- memory review
- benchmark history
- cost optimization suggestions
- model-routing improvements
- new tool suggestions
- backup/export snapshots
- Personal AI version history

## North-star business metrics

Track:

- time to first useful result
- activation rate
- first-skill completion
- benchmark improvement
- week-1 retention
- month-1 retention
- paid conversion
- upgrade rate
- churn
- average revenue per account
- referral rate
- marketplace purchases
- support contacts per active account
- successful outcomes per dollar of model/infrastructure cost

## Launch rule

Do not scale advertising aggressively until the product can demonstrate:

1. beginner can complete onboarding without assistance;
2. first personalized result works reliably;
3. permissions are understandable;
4. upgrade flow is truthful;
5. billing entitlements are enforced;
6. deletion/export works;
7. core benchmark suite passes;
8. failure recovery is understandable;
9. support documentation exists;
10. analytics can distinguish real customers from demos/simulations.

The goal is to make DreamCo **easy to understand, easy to try, easy to buy, easy to keep, and hard to outgrow**—without sacrificing truthfulness or user control.
