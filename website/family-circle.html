Safe places and alerts

Missing child emergency

If a child may be missing, act immediately. Do not wait for Buddy or rely only on GPS.

  1. Call local law enforcement or your local emergency number now.
  2. In the U.S., after reporting to law enforcement, call NCMEC at 1-800-843-5678. Outside the U.S., use your country’s missing-child hotline.
  3. Preserve the last known time, location, accuracy, device status, recent photo, clothing, contacts, and places searched. Do not publish a child’s live location publicly.
