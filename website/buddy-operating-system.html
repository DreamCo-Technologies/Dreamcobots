DreamCo · Buddy · Actions OS

One command center for the entire DreamCo intelligence system.

Actions is the control plane for goals, divisions, bots, capabilities, skills, memory, ontology, models, weights, tools, MCP, repositories, debugging, benchmarks, training, evidence and governed execution.

Operating principle: Buddy should optimize outcomes, not the number of agents. Every important action is separated into intent, context, planning, execution, verification and learning.
