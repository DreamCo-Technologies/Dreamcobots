Merchant Value Engine

Save the store owner money first. Monetize the value transparently.

How it works

Merchant consent → analyze transactions/expenses → compare authorized options → verify savings → merchant approval → measure actual savings → transparent DreamCo fee or revenue share.

What gets measured

  • Verified merchant savings
  • Merchant retained savings
  • DreamCo revenue
  • Merchant ROI
  • Transactions analyzed
  • Refunds and chargebacks
  • Time to savings
  • Cost to serve

Revenue options

SaaS, authorized referral/affiliate commissions, permitted transaction/service fees, verified-savings revenue sharing, marketplace commissions, and premium automation.

Trust rules

No hidden commissions, fabricated savings, unauthorized processor changes, or silent enrollment. Payment movement stays with approved providers and explicit merchant authorization.
