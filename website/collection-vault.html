User ownership: this vault is designed around user-controlled data. Connected apps must use authorized connectors, official exports, share/open-in mechanisms, or user uploads. Buddy should never scrape private app data, bypass app permissions, or silently copy everything from a device. Users choose what gets collected, where it is stored, what Buddy may read, and what can be deleted or exported.
