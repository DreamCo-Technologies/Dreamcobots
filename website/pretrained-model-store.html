How model purchases will work

  1. Choose a model and review its model card, benchmarks, license, and source-data rights summary.
  2. Check the required memory, runtime, storage, and expected compute for your device.
  3. Buy a license/download entitlement when checkout launches. The listing will clearly state whether commercial use, modification, and redistribution are permitted.
  4. Download the signed model package and verify its SHA-256 digest before loading it into Buddy, Ollama, or another supported runtime.

Local inference uses your own compute and electricity. DreamCo will not charge hosted-inference fees for work performed entirely on your hardware. Hosted inference, if offered, will be priced separately by usage.
