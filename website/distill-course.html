Targets and temperature

Hard target: one correct label. Student only learns the winner.

Soft target: the teacher's spread over answers. Student learns which answers were close.

Temperature: above 1 flattens the spread. At 1 you keep the teacher's sharpness. Below 1 peaks it.

Example: teacher says refund 0.7, exchange 0.2. Hard target teaches only refund. Soft target at temperature 2 also teaches that exchange was reasonable.

What you can predict

  • Next letter or token, for language.
  • Next candle: up, down, flat. Same loop, different labels. Not a broker and not proof of profit.
  • Next action: buy, hold, sell, or route-to-bot.
  • Next number: price, score, latency.
  • Next span: several tokens or several bars at once.

Benchmarks in the distill loop

Hourly: 20 held-out prompts, word or label overlap vs teacher, latency, and one failure from the last hour.

Daily: full holdout set, teacher-gap report, regression on yesterday's failures, and a decision: keep, roll back, or collect more teacher data.

Log each run. Do not train on the benchmark set.

Weight training ideas

  • KL between student and softened teacher, plus a hard-label term.
  • MSE on a hint layer if you have features.
  • LoRA on the student so you do not touch every weight.
  • Sweep temperature 1, 2, 4. Keep the one that wins the holdout.
  • Stop if the daily holdout drops. Roll back to the last good checkpoint.

These are plans. This page does not run a trainer.
