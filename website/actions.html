Data & personalization boundary

Make Buddy personal without turning user data into a free-for-all

Buddy should be able to organize user-authorized app exports, files, documents, images, notes, audio, video, contacts, calendars, messages, business records, and structured data. The default architecture is read-only-first, purpose-limited, provenance-aware, exportable, deletable, and reversible. Sensitive information requires a specific user-approved purpose rather than “collect everything possible.”
