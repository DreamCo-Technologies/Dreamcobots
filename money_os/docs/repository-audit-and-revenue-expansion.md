# DreamCo repository revenue expansion map

## Audit snapshot
The repository is already broad: it contains agent memory, CI/health automation, bot definitions, adaptive learning, autonomous-fleet/orchestration concepts, capital allocation, analytics, commodity/algo trading, lead generation, grants/contracts, real estate, jobs, software, revenue validation, and the new Money OS layer. The existing repository tree is large, so this document treats monetization as a cross-cutting capability rather than creating another disconnected bot silo.

## New revenue lanes to add to the common pipeline

1. **B2B RFP/RFQ matcher** — find procurement requests, normalize requirements, match DreamCo/customer capabilities, score contract value, and sell qualified leads or proposal services.
2. **Local-service lead exchange** — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, towing, junk removal, moving, auto repair, mobile mechanics. Revenue: qualified lead fees or subscriptions.
3. **Commercial vacancy intelligence** — detect vacant retail/industrial/office properties and route opportunities to brokers, contractors, property managers, and investors.
4. **Equipment arbitrage** — compare used equipment auction prices, dealer asking prices, transport costs, repair estimates, and resale comps. Revenue: leads, memberships, or transaction fees.
5. **Parts-price arbitrage** — identify large price spreads for legitimate automotive/industrial parts using authorized catalogs; monetize through affiliate or B2B sourcing fees.
6. **Business acquisition scout** — monitor businesses for sale, estimate normalized cash flow from public listing data, and match qualified buyers. Revenue: subscription or permitted referral fees.
7. **Expired-domain / digital-asset scout** — identify domains and digital assets with measurable commercial signals; monetize via subscription or brokerage where permitted.
8. **Unused-license/software savings audit** — help businesses identify redundant SaaS spend from user-provided billing/export data; monetize via audit fees or savings-share agreements.
9. **Utility/telecom bill audit** — parse user-provided bills, identify rate-plan anomalies and negotiable savings, and monetize via service fees or partner referrals.
10. **Insurance-shopping lead router** — identify users requesting quotes and route them only to appropriately licensed/authorized partners; revenue: permitted lead fees.
11. **Moving/storage arbitrage** — compare storage auctions, moving inventory, truck rentals, and local resale demand; revenue via leads/affiliate relationships.
12. **Auto opportunity engine** — combine vehicle listings, repair estimates, parts costs, recall/public-history data, and resale comps to flag underpriced vehicles; monetization through subscription/qualified leads.
13. **Contractor bid assistant** — convert customer requests into scopes, material lists, labor estimates, and bid packages. Revenue: SaaS subscription.
14. **Invoice recovery / AR scout** — analyze user-provided accounts-receivable data for overdue invoices and workflow opportunities. Revenue: SaaS or service fee.
15. **Tax-credit / incentive navigator** — discover official programs and match eligibility using user-provided business facts; revenue: subscription or qualified professional referral.
16. **Energy rebate finder** — match official utility/state/federal rebates to projects; revenue via lead/referral or premium subscription.
17. **Warranty/recall recovery assistant** — match user-provided purchases/serials against official warranty and recall programs; monetize through premium service.
18. **Subscription cancellation/savings assistant** — identify recurring charges from user-provided statements and produce cancellation/switch plans; subscription revenue.
19. **Freelance bid intelligence** — monitor authorized marketplaces for high-value jobs, score fit, generate proposal drafts, and track outcomes; subscription revenue.
20. **Creator sponsorship matcher** — match creators/businesses with sponsorship opportunities and track deliverables; subscription or marketplace fee.
21. **Digital product marketplace** — let DreamCo users sell templates, calculators, prompts, spreadsheets, training, and software; DreamCo takes a permitted marketplace fee.
22. **Micro-SaaS builder lane** — turn validated repeated workflows from existing bots into small paid SaaS products; recurring subscription revenue.
23. **Data-quality-as-a-service** — sell normalized, deduplicated, freshness-scored business opportunity datasets where collection and licensing rights permit it.
24. **API monetization layer** — expose verified opportunity scores, price history, lead qualification, and document extraction as metered APIs.
25. **Enterprise automation plans** — package DreamCo bots as managed automations with setup, monthly platform, usage, and support fees.

## Money OS rule
Every new lane must implement: source adapter -> normalization -> verification -> economics -> ranking -> monetization attribution -> user action -> outcome tracking -> revenue reconciliation -> observability.

## No fabricated inventory
The system must not invent live prices, deals, lawsuits, grants, jobs, leads, earnings, or resale values. Opportunity records require source and freshness metadata. Estimated profit must remain separate from realized profit.

## Priority order
P0: B2B RFP/RFQ, local-service leads, contractor bid assistant, business acquisition scout, utility/telecom audit, energy rebate finder.
P1: equipment/parts arbitrage, auto opportunity engine, commercial vacancy intelligence, SaaS savings audit, digital product marketplace.
P2: API monetization, enterprise plans, data products, creator sponsorships, micro-SaaS extraction.
