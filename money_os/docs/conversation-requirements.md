# Approved Money OS requirements captured from the build discussion

- Combine settlement/class-action discovery with deal, discount, coupon, price-drop, extreme-clearance, receipt-reward, and resale discovery.
- Compare opportunity sources and surface the best verified option rather than relying on one app/provider.
- Track price history and alert on meaningful drops.
- Support extreme-clearance/penny-deal discovery where an authorized source exposes the data.
- Analyze receipts and match eligible rewards/cashback; external submission must remain user-confirmed unless an authorized API permits automation.
- Find resale opportunities and calculate estimated net profit after purchase cost, coupon/cashback, shipping, tax, marketplace fees, and expected resale value.
- Discover official low-proof/low-documentation settlement opportunities, verify eligibility and deadlines, and provide a guided claim workflow.
- Integrate existing DreamCo revenue bots through a common registry and orchestration contract.
- Include dashboards for opportunities, earnings, referrals, claims, receipts, deals, flips, jobs/gigs, grants/contracts, and system health.
- Add monetization attribution for subscriptions, affiliate commissions, qualified leads, services, and permitted marketplace fees.
- Add verification, confidence, freshness, observability, retries, failure routing, and revenue reconciliation.
- Keep the production system compliant with source terms and prohibit credential abuse, CAPTCHA bypass, fake claims, fake reviews, and unauthorized submissions.
