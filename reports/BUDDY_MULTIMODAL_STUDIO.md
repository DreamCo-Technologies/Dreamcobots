## Hollywood Production Group

Cinema-production workflow and evidence gates. Final picture and sound quality must be demonstrated by rendered assets and master quality control; a plan or preview is not a finished film.

- `showrunner`: Showrunner and producer (`creative-brief-gen`)
- `writing`: Story and screenplay (`video-script`)
- `direction`: Direction and previs (`video-script`)
- `casting`: Casting and synthetic actors (`photo-video-app-bot`)
- `cinematography`: Cinematography and virtual production (`immersive-xr`)
- `production_design`: World and production design (`3d-asset-mgr`)
- `performance_animation`: Performance and animation (`immersive-xr`)
- `vfx`: VFX and simulation (`3d-asset-mgr`)
- `sound_music`: Sound, dialogue, and music (`music-production`)
- `editorial_finish`: Editorial, color, and finish (`video-editor-ai`)
- `accessibility_localization`: Accessibility and localization (`accessibility-auditor`)
- `rights_delivery`: Rights, safety, and delivery (`governance-dashboard`)

## Simulation Foundry

Visual and training simulations are decision aids. Physical fit, structural adequacy, safe repair, code compliance, equipment operation, and real-world outcomes require measured evidence and qualified review.

- `vehicle_service`: Vehicle service and repair
- `vehicle_customization`: Vehicle design and customization
- `building_design`: Home and building design
- `construction_practice`: Construction sequence practice
- `product_invention`: Product and invention prototype
- `equipment_training`: Equipment and job training
- `science_learning`: Science and classroom lab
- `business_operations`: Business and operations
- `custom`: Custom simulation

## Honest Media Readiness

The studio never calls a voice or likeness asset rendered until a configured engine returns an asset reference. Capture, consent, and local previews work independently of a paid service.

## Guardrails

- self-owned adult voice and likeness only
- no voice or likeness cloning of minors
- synthetic-media label cannot be disabled
- raw biometric media stays local or in an encrypted owner vault
- consent can be revoked
- publishing requires owner approval
- outside models are optional
