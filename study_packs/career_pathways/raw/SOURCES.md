# Sources (O*NET 31.0 pinned; dates are America/Chicago download dates)

- O*NET 31.0 Database (CSV, `db_31_0_csv`), U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA), CC BY 4.0. https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_files/database/db_31_0_csv/
  O*NET 31.0 splits the former Skills domain into Essential Skills (`essential_skills.csv`, used here) and Transferable Skills (`transferable_skills.csv`, not used).
  O*NET 31.0 Job Zones report a combined "Job Zone 1-2" (stored as 2 in `job_zones.csv`).
- CIP 2020 to O*NET-SOC 2019 crosswalk (O*NET Resource Center Crosswalk Files, USDOL/ETA, CC BY 4.0). https://www.onetcenter.org/crosswalks/cip/Education_CIP_to_ONET_SOC.xlsx
