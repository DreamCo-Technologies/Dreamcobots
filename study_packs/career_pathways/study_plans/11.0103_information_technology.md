## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Critical Thinking, Reading Comprehension, Active Listening.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Computers and Electronics, Mathematics, Engineering and Technology.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Software Quality Assurance Analysts and Testers; Computer Systems Analysts; Software Developers, with further work in Design, Telecommunications and applied practice in Speaking, Writing, Active Learning.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Software Quality Assurance Analysts and Testers; Computer Systems Analysts; Software Developers, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Customer and Personal Service (3.03): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Education and Training (2.85): teaching and training others is built through presenting, tutoring and peer teaching in projects rather than a dedicated course.
- Administration and Management (2.84): management and planning knowledge matters most after several years of experience, so the outline gives it no course slot (a management elective is optional).
