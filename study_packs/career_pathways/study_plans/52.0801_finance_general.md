## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Economics and Accounting (principles of economics and financial accounting); Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Budget Analysts; Credit Analysts; Loan Officers: Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles); Administration and Management (a management or organizational-behavior course plus a role leading a student project team); Law and Government (law, public policy, and regulation courses). Keep building Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Budget Analysts; Credit Analysts; Loan Officers; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows English Language, Economics and Accounting.
