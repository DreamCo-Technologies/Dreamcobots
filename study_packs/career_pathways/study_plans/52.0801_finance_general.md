## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking, Speaking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Economics and Accounting, Mathematics.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Customer and Personal Service, Administration and Management, Law and Government, plus Active Listening, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Chief Executives; build a portfolio showing English Language, Economics and Accounting.
