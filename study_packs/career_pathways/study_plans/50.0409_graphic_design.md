## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Active Listening, Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Computers and Electronics, English Language, Design.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Communications and Media, Fine Arts, Customer and Personal Service, plus Speaking, Active Learning through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Web and Digital Interface Designers; build a portfolio showing Computers and Electronics, English Language.
