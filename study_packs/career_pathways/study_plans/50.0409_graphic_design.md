## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Computers and Electronics (programming, data structures, and computer-systems coursework); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Design (technical drawing, CAD, or design-studio coursework).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Special Effects Artists and Animators; Graphic Designers; Web and Digital Interface Designers: Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles); Communications and Media (media writing and digital media production courses); Sales and Marketing (principles of marketing and consumer behavior). Keep building Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference); Active Learning (learning one new tool or method each term and documenting it) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Special Effects Artists and Animators; Graphic Designers; Web and Digital Interface Designers; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Computers and Electronics, English Language.
