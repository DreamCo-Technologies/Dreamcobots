## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Active Listening, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Public Safety and Security, Law and Government, English Language.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Administration and Management, Customer and Personal Service, Psychology, plus Monitoring, Reading Comprehension through projects.
