## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Public Safety and Security (safety, security, and risk-management coursework); Law and Government (law, public policy, and regulation courses); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for First-Line Supervisors of Correctional Officers: Administration and Management (a management or organizational-behavior course plus a role leading a student project team); Psychology (introductory and applied psychology courses); Administrative (records, scheduling, and office-software practice (e.g., an information-systems or business-communication course)). Keep building Monitoring (checking your own work against a checklist and tracking project progress); Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at First-Line Supervisors of Correctional Officers; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Public Safety and Security, Law and Government.
