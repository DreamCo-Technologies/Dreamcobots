## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Critical Thinking, Reading Comprehension, Active Listening.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Computers and Electronics, Engineering and Technology, English Language.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Mathematics, Design, Customer and Personal Service, plus Speaking, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Architectural and Engineering Managers; build a portfolio showing Computers and Electronics, Engineering and Technology.
