## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Active Listening, Speaking, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Law and Government, Administration and Management, Customer and Personal Service.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Compliance Officers; Labor Relations Specialists; Regulatory Affairs Specialists, with further work in Computers and Electronics, Administrative, Public Safety and Security and applied practice in Writing, Critical Thinking, Active Learning.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Compliance Officers; Labor Relations Specialists; Regulatory Affairs Specialists, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.
