## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Active Listening, Speaking, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Psychology, Therapy and Counseling, English Language.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Customer and Personal Service, Sociology and Anthropology, Education and Training, plus Reading Comprehension, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Social and Community Service Managers; build a portfolio showing Psychology, Therapy and Counseling.
