## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension, Active Listening, Speaking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Education and Training, Computers and Electronics, Communications and Media.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Librarians and Media Collections Specialists, with further work in Public Safety and Security, Law and Government and applied practice in Critical Thinking, Writing, Monitoring.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Librarians and Media Collections Specialists, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Customer and Personal Service (4.41): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Administration and Management (3.59): management and planning knowledge matters most after several years of experience, so the outline gives it no course slot (a management elective is optional).
- Administrative (3.52): office procedures and records handling; usually learned on the job, so it gets no course slot.
- Personnel and Human Resources (2.89): staffing and HR knowledge matters mainly for supervisory roles later in a career, so it gets no course slot.
