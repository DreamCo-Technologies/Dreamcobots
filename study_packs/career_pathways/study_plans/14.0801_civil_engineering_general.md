## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Reading Comprehension, Speaking, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Engineering and Technology, Design, Mathematics.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in English Language, Physics, Computers and Electronics, plus Active Listening, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Architectural and Engineering Managers; build a portfolio showing Engineering and Technology, Design.
