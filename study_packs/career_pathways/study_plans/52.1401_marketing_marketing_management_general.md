## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Active Listening, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Sales and Marketing, Customer and Personal Service.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Administration and Management, Communications and Media, Computers and Electronics, plus Critical Thinking, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Advertising and Promotions Managers; build a portfolio showing English Language, Sales and Marketing.
