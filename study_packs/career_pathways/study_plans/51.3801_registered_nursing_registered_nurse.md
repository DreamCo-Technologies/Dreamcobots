## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Active Listening, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Medicine and Dentistry, Psychology, English Language.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Customer and Personal Service, Education and Training, Therapy and Counseling, plus Critical Thinking, Monitoring through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Nursing Instructors and Teachers, Postsecondary; build a portfolio showing Medicine and Dentistry, Psychology.
