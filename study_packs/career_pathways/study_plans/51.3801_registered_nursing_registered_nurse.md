## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Medicine and Dentistry (anatomy, physiology, and pharmacology coursework); Psychology (introductory and applied psychology courses); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Registered Nurses; Acute Care Nurses; Critical Care Nurses: Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles); Education and Training (an instructional-methods course or peer-tutoring / teaching-assistant work); Therapy and Counseling (counseling-methods coursework with a supervised practicum). Keep building Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Monitoring (checking your own work against a checklist and tracking project progress) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Registered Nurses; Acute Care Nurses; Critical Care Nurses; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Medicine and Dentistry, Psychology.
