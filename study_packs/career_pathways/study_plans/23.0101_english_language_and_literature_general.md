## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Reading Comprehension, Writing, Speaking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Education and Training, Communications and Media.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Computers and Electronics, Customer and Personal Service, Sociology and Anthropology, plus Active Listening, Critical Thinking through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at English Language and Literature Teachers, Postsecondary; build a portfolio showing English Language, Education and Training.
