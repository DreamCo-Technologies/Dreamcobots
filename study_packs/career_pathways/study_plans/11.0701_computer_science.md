## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Computers and Electronics (programming, data structures, and computer-systems coursework); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Engineering and Technology (engineering fundamentals and applied-technology courses).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Software Quality Assurance Analysts and Testers; Information Security Analysts; Database Architects: Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics); Design (technical drawing, CAD, or design-studio coursework); Administration and Management (a management or organizational-behavior course plus a role leading a student project team). Keep building Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle); Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Software Quality Assurance Analysts and Testers; Information Security Analysts; Database Architects; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Computers and Electronics, English Language.
