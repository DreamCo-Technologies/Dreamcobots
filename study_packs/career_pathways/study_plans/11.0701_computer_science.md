## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Critical Thinking, Reading Comprehension, Active Listening.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Computers and Electronics, English Language, Engineering and Technology.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Mathematics, Customer and Personal Service, Administration and Management, plus Writing, Speaking through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Computer and Information Systems Managers; build a portfolio showing Computers and Electronics, English Language.
