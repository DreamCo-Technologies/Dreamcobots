## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Active Listening, Critical Thinking, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Public Safety and Security, Law and Government.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at First-Line Supervisors of Security Workers, with further work in Computers and Electronics, Psychology and applied practice in Speaking, Monitoring, Writing.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at First-Line Supervisors of Security Workers, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Administration and Management (4.64): management and planning knowledge matters most after several years of experience, so the outline gives it no course slot (a management elective is optional).
- Customer and Personal Service (4.43): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Personnel and Human Resources (4.41): staffing and HR knowledge matters mainly for supervisory roles later in a career, so it gets no course slot.
- Administrative (4.14): office procedures and records handling; usually learned on the job, so it gets no course slot.
- Education and Training (3.92): teaching and training others is built through presenting, tutoring and peer teaching in projects rather than a dedicated course.
