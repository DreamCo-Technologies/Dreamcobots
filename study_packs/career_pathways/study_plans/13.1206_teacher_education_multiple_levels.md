## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Learning Strategies, Speaking, Active Listening.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Education and Training, Psychology, Mathematics.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Elementary School Teachers, Except Special Education; Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education; Secondary School Teachers, Except Special and Career/Technical Education, with further work in Computers and Electronics, Sociology and Anthropology, Public Safety and Security and applied practice in Monitoring, Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Elementary School Teachers, Except Special Education; Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education; Secondary School Teachers, Except Special and Career/Technical Education, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Customer and Personal Service (3.68): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
