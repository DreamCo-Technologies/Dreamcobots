## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Learning Strategies, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Education and Training, Psychology.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Mathematics, Customer and Personal Service, Administration and Management, plus Active Listening, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Education Teachers, Postsecondary; build a portfolio showing English Language, Education and Training.
