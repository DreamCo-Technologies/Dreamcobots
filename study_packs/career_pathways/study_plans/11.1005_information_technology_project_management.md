## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking, Active Listening.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Computers and Electronics, Engineering and Technology, Telecommunications.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Information Security Analysts; Project Management Specialists; Penetration Testers, with further work in Mathematics, Communications and Media and applied practice in Writing, Monitoring, Speaking.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Information Security Analysts; Project Management Specialists; Penetration Testers, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Customer and Personal Service (3.34): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Administration and Management (3.23): management and planning knowledge matters most after several years of experience, so the outline gives it no course slot (a management elective is optional).
- Administrative (2.95): office procedures and records handling; usually learned on the job, so it gets no course slot.
