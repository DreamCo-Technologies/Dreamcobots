2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Education and Training, Mathematics, Computers and Electronics.

- Personnel and Human Resources (3.41): staffing and HR knowledge matters mainly for supervisory roles later in a career, so it gets no course slot.
