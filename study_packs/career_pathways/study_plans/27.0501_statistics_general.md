## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Computers and Electronics (programming, data structures, and computer-systems coursework).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Data Scientists; Actuaries; Business Intelligence Analysts: Economics and Accounting (principles of economics and financial accounting); Administration and Management (a management or organizational-behavior course plus a role leading a student project team); Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles). Keep building Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference); Mathematics (regular quantitative problem sets) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Data Scientists; Actuaries; Business Intelligence Analysts; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Mathematics, English Language.
