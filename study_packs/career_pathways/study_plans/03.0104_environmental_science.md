## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Law and Government (law, public policy, and regulation courses); Computers and Electronics (programming, data structures, and computer-systems coursework).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Environmental Scientists and Specialists, Including Health; Environmental Science and Protection Technicians, Including Health; Compliance Officers: Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles); Public Safety and Security (safety, security, and risk-management coursework); Administration and Management (a management or organizational-behavior course plus a role leading a student project team). Keep building Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Environmental Scientists and Specialists, Including Health; Environmental Science and Protection Technicians, Including Health; Compliance Officers; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows English Language, Law and Government.
