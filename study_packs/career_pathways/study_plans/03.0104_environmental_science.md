## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Reading Comprehension, Active Listening, Speaking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Law and Government, Computers and Electronics.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Customer and Personal Service, Mathematics, Biology, plus Critical Thinking, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Compliance Officers; build a portfolio showing English Language, Law and Government.
