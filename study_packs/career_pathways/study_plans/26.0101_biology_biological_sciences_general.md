## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Reading Comprehension, Active Listening, Speaking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Biology, English Language, Computers and Electronics.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Mathematics, Education and Training, Chemistry, plus Critical Thinking, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Natural Sciences Managers; build a portfolio showing Biology, English Language.
