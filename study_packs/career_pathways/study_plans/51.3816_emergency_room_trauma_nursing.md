## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Speaking, Active Listening, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Medicine and Dentistry, Psychology, Therapy and Counseling.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Registered Nurses; Acute Care Nurses; Critical Care Nurses, with further work in Biology, Mathematics and applied practice in Reading Comprehension, Monitoring, Active Learning.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Registered Nurses; Acute Care Nurses; Critical Care Nurses, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Customer and Personal Service (4.17): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Education and Training (3.83): teaching and training others is built through presenting, tutoring and peer teaching in projects rather than a dedicated course.
