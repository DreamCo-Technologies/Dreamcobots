## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Engineering and Technology (engineering fundamentals and applied-technology courses); Computers and Electronics (programming, data structures, and computer-systems coursework); Design (technical drawing, CAD, or design-studio coursework).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Electronics Engineers, Except Computer; Electrical Engineers; Computer Hardware Engineers: Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Physics (calculus-based physics with labs). Keep building Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Electronics Engineers, Except Computer; Electrical Engineers; Computer Hardware Engineers; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Engineering and Technology, Computers and Electronics.
