## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics); Computers and Electronics (programming, data structures, and computer-systems coursework).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Social Science Research Assistants; Secondary School Teachers, Except Special and Career/Technical Education: Education and Training (an instructional-methods course or peer-tutoring / teaching-assistant work); Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles); Administrative (records, scheduling, and office-software practice (e.g., an information-systems or business-communication course)). Keep building Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle); Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Social Science Research Assistants; Secondary School Teachers, Except Special and Career/Technical Education; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows English Language, Mathematics.
