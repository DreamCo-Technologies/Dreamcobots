## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Active Listening, Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Design, Computers and Electronics, Communications and Media.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Graphic Designers; Web and Digital Interface Designers; Commercial and Industrial Designers, with further work in Production and Processing, Engineering and Technology and applied practice in Speaking, Active Learning, Writing.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Graphic Designers; Web and Digital Interface Designers; Commercial and Industrial Designers, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

- Customer and Personal Service (3.07): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Administration and Management (2.96): management and planning knowledge matters most after several years of experience, so the outline gives it no course slot (a management elective is optional).
