## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Writing, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Communications and Media, Computers and Electronics.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Sales and Marketing, Customer and Personal Service, Administration and Management, plus Active Listening, Critical Thinking through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Public Relations Managers; build a portfolio showing English Language, Communications and Media.
