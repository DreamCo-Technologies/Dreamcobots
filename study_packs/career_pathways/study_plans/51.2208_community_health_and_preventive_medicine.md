## 4-year outline (DreamCo-generated template, not O*NET data; element names only, no per-major course mapping)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education, including English Language, plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Active Listening, Critical Thinking, Speaking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Courses in the major that build the highest-rated knowledge areas: Medicine and Dentistry, Psychology, Therapy and Counseling.
3. Year 3, depth. Electives aimed at Community Health Workers, with further work in Computers and Electronics, Public Safety and Security and applied practice in Active Learning, Reading Comprehension, Monitoring.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Community Health Workers, with a portfolio drawn from the practice exercises below.

Knowledge areas from the top list that the outline deliberately does not cover:

- Customer and Personal Service (4.31): service and client-relations knowledge; the outline leaves it to internships, part-time work and the practice tasks rather than a course slot.
- Education and Training (3.5): teaching and training others is built through presenting, tutoring and peer teaching in projects rather than a dedicated course.
- Administration and Management (3.12): management and planning knowledge matters most after several years of experience, so the outline gives it no course slot (a management elective is optional).
