## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking, Active Listening.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Economics and Accounting, Mathematics.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Customer and Personal Service, Law and Government, Administration and Management, plus Speaking, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Accountants and Auditors; build a portfolio showing English Language, Economics and Accounting.
