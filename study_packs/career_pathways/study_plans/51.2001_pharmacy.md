## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Reading Comprehension, Critical Thinking.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in English Language, Education and Training, Medicine and Dentistry.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Customer and Personal Service, Administration and Management, Biology, plus Active Listening, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Medical and Health Services Managers; build a portfolio showing English Language, Education and Training.
