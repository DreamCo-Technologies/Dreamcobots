## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference); Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Education and Training (an instructional-methods course or peer-tutoring / teaching-assistant work); Medicine and Dentistry (anatomy, physiology, and pharmacology coursework).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Pharmacists: Biology (the biology lecture-and-lab sequence); Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics); Chemistry (general and organic chemistry with labs). Keep building Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Pharmacists; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows English Language, Education and Training.
