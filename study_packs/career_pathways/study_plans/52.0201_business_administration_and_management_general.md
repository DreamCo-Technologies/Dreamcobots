## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in Speaking, Active Listening, Reading Comprehension.
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Introductory courses in Administration and Management, English Language, Customer and Personal Service.
3. Year 3, depth. Advanced courses in Personnel and Human Resources, Education and Training, Computers and Electronics, plus Critical Thinking, Writing through projects.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Chief Executives; build a portfolio showing Administration and Management, English Language.
