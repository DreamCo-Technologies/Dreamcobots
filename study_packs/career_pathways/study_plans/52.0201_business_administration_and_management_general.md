## 4-year outline (DreamCo-derived guidance, not O*NET data)

1. Year 1, foundations. General education plus deliberate practice in the top-rated skills: Speaking (presenting work in class or at a student conference); Active Listening (paraphrasing teammates' points before responding in group work); Reading Comprehension (reading and summarizing technical or primary sources each week).
2. Year 2, core knowledge. Build the highest-rated knowledge areas for this major: Administration and Management (a management or organizational-behavior course plus a role leading a student project team); English Language (advanced composition and technical or professional writing); Customer and Personal Service (client-facing experience such as tutoring, help-desk, or service-learning roles).
3. Year 3, depth toward the entry targets. Prioritize knowledge rated highest for Project Management Specialists; Management Analysts; Compensation, Benefits, and Job Analysis Specialists: Personnel and Human Resources (human resource management coursework); Mathematics (college mathematics through calculus and statistics); Administrative (records, scheduling, and office-software practice (e.g., an information-systems or business-communication course)). Keep building Critical Thinking (comparing alternative solutions in case studies and stating trade-offs); Writing (writing memos or lab reports with a feedback-and-revision cycle) through project work.
4. Year 4, launch. Internship or capstone aimed at Project Management Specialists; Management Analysts; Compensation, Benefits, and Job Analysis Specialists; build a portfolio from the practice tasks below that shows Administration and Management, English Language.
